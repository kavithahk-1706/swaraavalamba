What Swarāvalamba Does

Hand Gesture Tracking

Uses your webcam with MediaPipe to detect tālam kriyā in real time — finger counts, beats, and palm flips across air, palm, and lap modes.

Swara Detection

Listens to your singing through the mic and maps your pitch to the nearest Carnatic swara, with cents deviation for microtonal feedback.

Combined Scoring

Scores both your tālam accuracy and swara precision per beat, with weighted partial credit — ±1 semitone tolerance for fast passages.

Exercise Library

Sarali and dātu swara exercises with 1st, 2nd, and 3rd speed support, in the rāga of your choice.

Shruti & Tanpura

Set your base shruti (Sa) from C to B, with a built-in tanpura drone that pitch-shifts to match — practice anywhere, anytime.

Detailed Results

After each practice run, see per-swara accuracy with color-coded results, tala cycle breakdowns, and an overall grade.

Ready to Practice?

All you need is a webcam, a microphone, and your voice. No downloads, no installs — it all runs in your browser.
